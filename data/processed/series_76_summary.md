# Series 76 Electric Actuators

> Source: `docs/assesment/series_76_summary.pdf` (generated by `backend/pipeline/summary_to_md.py`).

The data provided pertains to the Series 76, a line of heavy-duty electric actuators. These devices are critical components in industrial automation, used to remotely open, close, or precisely control valves and dampers in a wide range of applications, from HVAC systems to industrial processing plants.

## Understanding the Data Tables

The provided tables contain the Electrical Data for the Series 76 actuators. This data is essential for engineers and technicians to select the correct actuator model for a specific application based on power availability and performance requirements.

The data is segmented based on several key criteria:

1. **Enclosure Type:**
   - **Weatherproof:** Designed for general industrial use, protected against dust, rain, and environmental elements (compliant with CSA, CE, & UKCA standards).
   - **Explosionproof:** Built with a more robust enclosure to be safely operated in hazardous environments where flammable gases or dust may be present (compliant with CSA, IECEx/ATEX standards).
2. **Power Supply:**
   - **24V AC/DC:** Low voltage direct or alternating current.
   - **110V Single Phase:** Standard North American voltage.
   - **220V Single Phase:** Common international voltage.
3. **Application Type:**
   - **On/Off:** The actuator operates in two positions: fully open or fully closed.
   - **Modulating:** The actuator can be controlled to stop at any point in its 90-degree rotation, allowing for precise flow control.

## Data Fields in the Tables

- **Base Part Number:** The unique identifier for each actuator model (e.g., 761A00-11380000/A).
- **Output Torque:** The rotational force the actuator can produce, measured in in-lbs (inch-pounds) and Nm (Newton-meters). This is a primary performance metric.
- **Duty Cycle (S4%):** The percentage of time the actuator can be in operation over a given cycle without overheating.
- **Operating Speed (Sec):** The time it takes for the actuator to complete a full 90-degree turn. For AC models, this can vary based on the power frequency (50 Hz vs. 60 Hz).
- **Full Load Current (FLA):** The electric current (in Amps) drawn by the motor under its maximum rated load.
- **Locked Rotor Current (LRA):** The current drawn by the motor at startup, which is significantly higher than the FLA.
- **Motor Power:** The power rating of the internal motor, measured in Watts.
